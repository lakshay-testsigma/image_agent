# Style inspiration (ideas, not rules)

Starting points to mix, adapt or ignore. The user's direction always wins.

**Clean catalog.** Seamless white (#FFFFFF) or warm-grey sweep, soft even key light, gentle grounded shadow, true colour, whole product visible, centred. Marketplace-safe.

**Luxury editorial.** Dark or deep-toned set, controlled hard light with sculpted highlights, polished stone, smoked glass or brushed metal, reflections, generous negative space, slow and still.

**Minimal lifestyle.** Natural window light, linen or light wood, one or two meaningful props, airy and calm, muted palette.

**Scandinavian / Japandi interior.** Pale oak, plaster walls, paper lamps, low furniture, morning light, an uncluttered room.

**Warm home moment.** Lived-in kitchen or living room, steam from a cup, a book left open, golden hour, a slightly imperfect styled mess.

**Flat lay.** Top-down on textured paper, terrazzo or fabric, arranged accessories, graphic spacing, soft overhead light.

**Macro detail.** Extreme close-up of texture, stitching, grain, engraving or mechanism. Shallow depth of field, raking light that reveals material.

**In use / hands.** Hands interacting with the product, which shows scale and function. Neutral manicure and simple sleeves keep focus on the product.

**On-model fashion.** Natural poses with real fabric drape, editorial location or studio cyclorama, front, back and three-quarter angles, plus a motion shot (walk or turn).

**Ghost mannequin / flat garment.** Hollow 3D garment form or a neatly folded flat lay on a neutral background. Shows cut and print clearly.

**Outdoor / adventure.** The product in its real environment (trail, beach, city street). Weather and atmosphere, wide lens, sense of place.

**Colour-block pop.** Saturated seamless paper backgrounds, a complementary colour pair, hard light with crisp shadows, a playful D2C look.

**Surreal / conceptual.** Floating products, oversized props, impossible architecture, sculptural plinths, dreamlike light. Good for campaigns, poor as the main catalog image.

**Seasonal / gifting.** Holiday, summer or wedding cues, wrapping, ribbons, festive light. Keep the product as the hero.

**Packaging and unboxing.** Box, tissue and inserts, half-opened reveal, hands lifting the product.

**Scale and spec-ready.** A clean scene with deliberate empty space and a known-size reference object, designed for dimension callouts added by overlay.

**Infographic hero.** Product on a soft gradient, space for 3–5 feature callouts with leader lines (added by overlay, using verified facts only).

Combine freely, for example "luxury editorial light + Japandi set + macro detail of the joinery".

## Beyond photography (mediums)

**3D render.** Clean CGI, soft global illumination, clay or glossy materials, floating objects, product exploded views.

**Isometric.** 30° grid, miniature worlds, tech and logistics stories, SaaS feature explainers.

**Flat vector / editorial illustration.** Bold shapes, limited palette, grain texture, explainer and blog headers.

**Line art / ink / engraving.** Monoline or cross-hatched, packaging and heritage brands, menus.

**Painterly.** Watercolour, gouache, oil, pastel. Travel posters, children's books, premium food and wine.

**Anime / comic / manga key visual.** Cel shading, dynamic angles, speed lines. Games, entertainment, youth brands.

**Pixel art / retro digital.** 8/16-bit, Y2K, vaporwave, synthwave. Gaming, music, nostalgia campaigns.

**Collage / mixed media.** Cut-paper, risograph, halftone, photo-montage. Fashion, culture, music, zines.

**Claymation / felt / papercraft.** Tactile, handmade, playful. Kids, food, friendly fintech.

**Swiss / Bauhaus / typographic poster.** Grids and strong geometry, leaving space for overlay typography.

**Blueprint / technical.** Schematic lines, callouts, engineering credibility. Tools, automotive, hardware.

**Data-viz / infographic backgrounds.** Calm gradients and shapes with room for verified numbers added by overlay.

**Cinematic.** Anamorphic flares, film grain, teal-orange or bleach-bypass grade, a still from a film that doesn't exist.

**Documentary / reportage.** Natural, imperfect, candid. NGOs, corporate culture, travel.

Any medium works with any subject. For `exact` fidelity in a stylised medium, either keep the subject photographic inside a stylised world, or composite the real subject with `overlay`.
